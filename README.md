## 📌 Project Overview
**RESQAI** is an AI-powered emergency healthcare system designed to act as the central nervous system for critical care. In medical emergencies, every minute matters. RESQAI automates and optimizes decision-making by dynamically analyzing emergency data, triaging patients, tracking live hospital resources, and routing ambulances to save lives without administrative delays.

---

## 🚨 The Core Problem
Emergency response systems currently suffer from major coordination gaps:
* Delayed ambulance dispatches and lack of real-time coordination between hospitals and ambulances.
* Absence of centralized tracking for hospital bed, ICU, and resource availability.
* Long waiting times and severely overcrowded emergency departments.

### Affected Stakeholders & Settings
* **Who it affects:** Patients requiring emergency care, ambulance operators, doctors, hospital staff, and government emergency agencies.
* **Where it occurs:** Government & private hospitals, accident sites, disaster zones, and both rural and urban emergency healthcare networks.

---

## 💡 The AI Solution & Key Features
An AI Agent is uniquely suited for this problem because it can evaluate data instantly, continuously, and without human error. Key capabilities include:

* **AI-Based Patient Triage:** Instantly prioritizes emergency cases based on severity.
* **Real-Time Resource Tracking:** Continuous live tracking of hospital beds, ICUs, and medical equipment.
* **Automatic Hospital Recommendation:** Matches and routes patients to the nearest suitable hospital with proven capacity.
* **Smart Ambulance Routing:** Directs drivers via optimal paths to reduce transit time.
* **Centralized Healthcare Command Dashboard:** Provides national and regional visibility across healthcare systems.

---

## 🎯 Target Impact
* **Reduces Emergency Response Times:** Cuts down delay from incident to treatment.
* **Saves More Lives:** Maximizes survival rates during the "golden hour."
* **Optimizes Resource Allocation:** Prevents overcrowding at major hospitals by distributing patients efficiently.

---

## 📈 Target Customers & Business Model
* **Target Customers:** Government and private hospitals, emergency ambulance providers, smart city healthcare initiatives, and disaster management authorities.
* **Business Model:** Delivered as a **Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)** subscription model for healthcare organizations, along with premium analytics, API integrations, and government service contracts.

---
